Questions to Ask Your Boat/Cruise

Before you go — email or call and ask:
  • Do you provide wetsuits? What thickness? (July is cooler season)
  • Do you provide snorkel gear (mask, snorkel, fins)? Can we bring our own?
  • Is there a rinse station / drying area for wet gear on board?
  • What's the power outlet situation in cabins? (charging cameras/GoPro)
  • Do you accommodate vegetarian meals? (notify dietary needs!)
  • What's the typical daily schedule? (wake time, number of activities)
  • Is there a kayak or paddleboard option?
  • Do we need to pay the Galapagos park fee ($100/person) separately at the airport?
  • Do we need to buy a Transit Control Card ($20/person) before departure?
  • Is there Wi-Fi on board? (usually no or very limited)
  • What's the luggage/weight limit for the inter-island flight?
  • Tipping policy for crew/naturalist guide?

Galapagos Fees & Logistics

Pay at the airport (not included in cruise price):
  • Galapagos National Park fee: $100/person (cash USD, paid on arrival in Galapagos)
  • Transit Control Card (TCT): $20/person (bought at Quito or Guayaquil airport before departure)
  • Bag inspection: Required at Quito airport — no fresh fruit, plants, or seeds allowed

What NOT to Bring

  • Non-reef-safe sunscreen (will be confiscated at park checkpoints)
  • Drones (banned in Galapagos National Park)
  • Fresh food/fruit (biosecurity — inspected at airport)
  • Plastic straws or single-use plastic bags
  • Perfume/cologne (attracts insects, disturbs wildlife)
  • White-soled shoes (can transfer organisms between islands — dark soles preferred)

Panoramic views of Cotopaxi, Sincholagua, Corazon, and Pasochoa. Helmets required for final stretch ($5pp). Starts 8am. Bring boxed lunch.

Vegetarian Tips

Watch out for hidden meat:
  • "Vegetable soup" often has chicken stock — ask "¿Tiene caldo de carne o pollo?"
  • Lard sometimes used for frying — ask "¿Usa manteca?"
  • Street food is mostly meat-based (except empanadas de viento and choclo)

At the Lodges & Galapagos

Both Cotopaxi lodges accommodate vegetarian — just mention "2 vegetarian guests" when booking. Highland cuisine (potatoes, quinoa, corn, cheese) is naturally veggie-heavy.

Galapagos: Notify your boat/cruise of vegetarian needs when you book. Less variety on islands than Quito — plan ahead.

Practical Info

Altitude

Quito: 2,850m (9,350 ft) — you'll feel it the first day.
Cotopaxi hike: Up to 4,864m (15,958 ft).

Tips: Hydrate aggressively. Go slow the first day. Coca tea helps. Avoid alcohol first night. Ibuprofen for headaches.

Money

  • Ecuador uses US Dollars — no currency exchange needed
  • ATMs available in Quito. Bring some cash for rural areas (Cotopaxi, markets)
  • Credit cards accepted at restaurants/hotels, not at markets or street food

Transport

  • Airport to hotel: ~35 min, Uber works great ($8-12)
  • In Quito: Uber everywhere ($2-5 per ride)
  • Cotopaxi: Private driver (~$100-150 round trip for 4) or lodge transfer

Packing Essentials

  • Layers — Quito is 15-20°C day, 8-10°C night
  • Warm jacket for Cotopaxi (can be near freezing at altitude)
  • Rain jacket (July is drier season but highland weather is unpredictable)
  • Sunscreen + sunglasses (equatorial sun + altitude = intense UV)
  • Hiking shoes/boots for Cotopaxi
  • Swimsuit (hot tub at lodge)
