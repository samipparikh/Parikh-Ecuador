Vegetarian Tips

Watch out for hidden meat:
  • "Vegetable soup" often has chicken stock — ask "¿Tiene caldo de carne o pollo?"
  • Lard sometimes used for frying — ask "¿Usa manteca?"
  • Street food is mostly meat-based (except empanadas de viento and choclo)

At the Lodges & Galapagos

Both Cotopaxi lodges accommodate vegetarian — just mention "2 vegetarian guests" when booking. Highland cuisine (potatoes, quinoa, corn, cheese) is naturally veggie-heavy.

Galapagos: Notify your boat/cruise of vegetarian needs when you book. Less variety on islands than Quito — plan ahead.

Practical Info

Altitude

Quito: 2,850m (9,350 ft) — you'll feel it the first day.
Cotopaxi hike: Up to 4,864m (15,958 ft).

Tips: Hydrate aggressively. Go slow the first day. Coca tea helps. Avoid alcohol first night. Ibuprofen for headaches.

Money

  • Ecuador uses US Dollars — no currency exchange needed
  • ATMs available in Quito. Bring some cash for rural areas (Cotopaxi, markets)
  • Credit cards accepted at restaurants/hotels, not at markets or street food

Transport

  • Airport to hotel: ~35 min, Uber works great ($8-12)
  • In Quito: Uber everywhere ($2-5 per ride)
  • Cotopaxi: Private driver (~$100-150 round trip for 4) or lodge transfer

Packing Essentials

  • Layers — Quito is 15-20°C day, 8-10°C night
  • Warm jacket for Cotopaxi (can be near freezing at altitude)
  • Rain jacket (July is drier season but highland weather is unpredictable)
  • Sunscreen + sunglasses (equatorial sun + altitude = intense UV)
  • Hiking shoes/boots for Cotopaxi
  • Swimsuit (hot tub at lodge)
